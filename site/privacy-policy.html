privacy

Yonder privacy policy. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Yonder is a podcast, audiobook and radio app. I made it, and I don't want your data.

the short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking and no analytics. Yonder doesn't count you, profile you or sell anything about you.
  • Everything you do in Yonder stays on your phone.

what stays on your phone

Your shows, books, stations, Smart Plays, what you've played, your settings and your downloads. They live on your phone and nowhere else. I can't see them.

who Yonder talks to, and why

Yonder has to reach the internet to get your shows. It only talks to these places, and only to do the job you asked for:

  • Podcast feeds and episode files. To check for new episodes, download them and show their cover art, Yonder asks each show's own website or host. That's the same as any podcast app.
  • Podcast Index and Apple's podcast directory. When you search for a show, look at what's popular, or paste or share a show link, Yonder asks them to find it. It sends what you typed or the link, and your country for the popular list, nothing else.
  • Radio Browser. When you search for a radio station. It sends your search.
  • LibriVox. When you search for an audiobook. It sends your search.
  • Spotify. Only when you paste or share a Spotify link. Yonder opens that show's public Spotify page to read its name, so it can find the same show elsewhere.
  • Radio stations you add. To play them, and to record shows you've asked it to record.

Like any website you visit, these places can see your phone's internet address when Yonder asks them for something. Yonder doesn't send them anything about you.

private feeds

Some feeds are private, like Patreon. Their links and any passwords stay on your phone. Yonder never shows them in logs or reports.

backups

Backups are files you make and keep. You choose where they go, like a folder on your phone or your Google Drive. They hold everything in Yonder, including private feed links and passwords, so you can add a password to lock them. I never see your backups.

feedback and crash reports

Yonder only sends feedback or a crash report if you choose to. It opens an email in your own email app, which you can read before you send. If you attach a log, private links, passwords and file names are taken out first.

Google Play

If you install Yonder from Google Play, Google handles the install and updates under its own privacy policy. Yonder doesn't add anything to that.

children

Yonder doesn't collect anything from anyone, including children.

changes

If this ever changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
